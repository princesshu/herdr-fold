herdr-fold themes

Each card is a sketch of the work window in the colors the package sets for that theme: herdr's sidebar on the left (Spaces, then the agents and sessions), the tab bar on top, Claude in the middle, on the right the sidebar (its row of views, Changes with a commit message and the Commit button) above the terminal, under the window the installer's loading sheep, and beside them the theme's app icon, herdr-fold's sheep. Claude Code's own colors (the diff's background, its status line) are the same in every theme.

Choosing: the installer asks for the card's number. You can switch any time later: Cmd + K, then Cmd + T (picker window), or in a herdr pane python3 ~/.config/herdr-fold/themes/apply.py 2 (the number or the name, e.g. vscode). Then press Cmd + Shift + , in Ghostty to reload.

You can also cycle through all 463 Ghostty themes live in a terminal (the terminal part only, without herdr's bars): /Applications/Ghostty.app/Contents/MacOS/ghostty +list-themes
