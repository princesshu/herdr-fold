herdr-fold themes

Each card is a sketch of the work window in the colors the package sets for that theme: herdr's sidebar on the left (Spaces, Sessions), the tab bar on top, Claude in the middle, on the right the sidebar (its views, the files, the Commit button) above the terminal, under the window the installer's loading sheep, and beside them the theme's app icon, herdr-fold's sheep. Claude Code's own colors (its orange logo, the diff background) are the same in every theme.

Choosing: the installer asks for the card's number. You can switch any time later: Cmd + K, then Cmd + T (picker window), or in a herdr pane python3 ~/.config/herdr-fold/themes/apply.py 2 (the number or the name, e.g. vscode). Then press Cmd + Shift + , in Ghostty to reload.

You can also cycle through all 463 Ghostty themes live in a terminal (the terminal part only, without herdr's bars): /Applications/Ghostty.app/Contents/MacOS/ghostty +list-themes
